### Demo 0.1 (in development)
- Region I, the Cajon Pass: Station Four, the convoy prologue, the road north, three Waylights, the cartel at the reservoir and the Heretic at the summit.
- Two operatives: Marcus Garrison and Sarah Kang.
- Four companions with signature weapons and levels.
- Day and night, with deadlier nights and infected packs.
- Save and load, gamepad support, full graphics and accessibility settings.

Regions II (Las Vegas) and III (Ground Zero) are being built for the demo.
