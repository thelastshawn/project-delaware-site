This is the first thing every child at a LANTERN station is taught, before letters, before numbers: *when the strain takes someone, they die.*

Not later. Not slowly. The person you knew is gone in the first hour, and what gets up afterwards is wearing them. It has their face and their clothes and sometimes, for a while, their walk. It has nothing else. Nobody has ever come back. Nobody ever will.

You learn to know them by the light. The strain glows in them, teal and green, along the veins and behind the eyes, brightest in the dark. Some grow crystal out of their shoulders and their skulls, white and sharp, with that same light in the core. A lantern-carrier sees a teal glow at the edge of the beam and is already moving.

---

They are not all the same.

Most are Husks: slow, heavy, many. The Frenzied are not slow. Some bloat with acid bile and spit it. Some swell into Spore Brutes three metres tall that come through walls instead of around them. Some wail, and when they wail, others answer.

The worst thing about them is not the teeth. It is that they come in numbers. Kill two at the edge of a town and you will hear the rest of the town wake up. They come out of the dark at a distance, in packs of eight and ten and fifteen, and something larger walks behind them.

At night, they are faster, and there are more of them. Everyone who has walked the road knows this. Everyone who forgot it is part of the road now.
