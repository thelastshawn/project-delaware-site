There is one place in the country the strain has never entered.

It is in Dover, Delaware, on the other side of everything. It is called Facility Zero, and it has been broadcasting the same three words on every frequency for five years. *Make for Facility Zero.* No infected has ever been inside its walls. It is the largest safe haven left, and the last good news anyone has.

For five years it was unreachable. Two thousand miles of dead country stood between the West Coast and Dover, and every region on the way belonged to a hive.

---

This autumn, LANTERN found a road.

It is not a safe road. It runs north through the Cajon Pass, up the old I-15 into Nevada, past the place where it all began, and then east across salt and plains and river country, through cities that still have something living in them, to the coal hills and the sea. Every region on it is held by an apex. Every apex has to fall before the road is open.

And the hives are moving. All of them, everywhere, are turning east.

LANTERN is sending one operative, with a small team: Marcus Garrison, who can't die, or Sarah Kang, who went into the dark once and came back half machine. Whichever one walks, the other will be on the LINK, a relay behind, talking them through it.

Five years. One road north, and then east, as far as it goes.

Keep the light on.
