The strain took Marcus Garrison and did not win.

Nobody knows why. LANTERN has spent five years trying to find out. He was infected like anyone else, early, in the worst of it. The fever came, and the fissures opened in his skin, glowing red like coals instead of teal like everyone else's. And then the fever broke, and he sat up, and he asked for water. He knew his name. He knew theirs.

He is the only one. As far as anyone knows, he will stay the only one. His blood has been drawn and drawn again. There is no cure in it.

---

He doesn't age. The people who knew him before have grey in their hair now, and he looks exactly as he did the summer it happened. Kovic, at Station Four, has a theory that he can't die of anything but violence. She doesn't say it to him.

The strain in him is a weapon, and he has learned to use it: a crescent of his own infection thrown like a blade, a shell of black flame he can pull over himself when the hive gets close. It costs him something every time. He never says what.

The people who served with him before still call him Gunny. Everyone else calls him LANTERN's lone wolf, because he works alone, because nobody else can go where he goes and come back.

He is the thing every apex is, and he is still a man. Every apex he meets on the road is the man he could have been. Most of them notice.
