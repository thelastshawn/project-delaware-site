Project DELAWARE is made by one person. That means every system, every model and every line of this site goes through the same pair of hands, and it means the budget is zero: free tools, free assets, and a lot of nights.

This month the game got **one art style**, locked for good: *grounded photoreal*. Real proportions, real materials, five years of dust and sun on everything. Anything that doesn't fit, whether a low-poly pack, a stylised body or an untextured box, gets replaced.

### A new way to build characters

Until now the characters were generic bodies with their concept art painted on, and they never quite looked like the art. The new pipeline builds each character straight from their concept sheet. It generates a full 3D model from the art, cleans it up, gives skin and cloth their own real materials, fits a skeleton to the concept's proportions, and skins it so the hands hold a rifle properly.

Marcus went through it first. The Heretic, the first apex, went through it this week. The rest of the cast is next, then every enemy on the road, three looks per enemy type so no two packs look the same.

### Cutscenes

The Heretic's scenes were recorded again with the new model. Mouths now open and close with every line, and the cameras follow the actors' real heads, so nothing important drifts out of frame.
